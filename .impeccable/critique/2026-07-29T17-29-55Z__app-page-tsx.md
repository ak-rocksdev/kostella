## Design Specificity Verdict

**The words are authored. The design is borrowed.**

Unmistakably Kostella: the H1 leads with the *mechanism* (ownership), not a benefit — a harder and better move than any competitor makes. "Kamar yang tampil di sini benar-benar kosong hari ini" is falsifiable, which in a fraud-fearing market beats any adjective.

Category-interchangeable: everything you can see. Full-bleed lifestyle photo, left scrim, white type left, floating white card right, four-stat strip below. Delete the Indonesian and this is Cove, Rukita, or Hmlet.

The damning specific, verified independently: **the `numeral` utility — Archivo Expanded 700, the brand's stated identity — is used nine times across this codebase and zero times in the hero.** The largest number above the fold is `Rp2.100.000`: the visitor's budget, not Kostella's identity. Meanwhile the opening frame rests on a photograph that PRODUCT.md states is not a Kostella property.

**Deterministic scan.** CLI detector: 0 findings, exit 0 — but Assessment B proved this is weak evidence by running a positive control (a deliberately bad file *was* flagged). For TSX the CLI is regex-based, not rendered. The in-browser detector on the same code found 14 findings at 1440 and 18 at 390. Exactly one lands in scope, at both widths: `all-caps-body` on the eyebrow — a **false positive**, since `tokens/typography.css:14` and `readme.md:22` both define ALL CAPS as the eyebrow's documented treatment, and readme.md:26 names this exact string as the canonical example. ProofBar: zero findings at both widths.

Out of scope but genuine, found by the detector and worth logging: `low-contrast 4.0:1` on the "Sisa 1" held badge in PropertyCard (`#b4531e` on `#f5e3d7`).

**Visual overlays:** injection succeeded; both live-server instances were stopped and verified down.

## Overall Impression

The copy is the best thing here and it is doing all the work alone. Both sections are structurally sound and measurably accessible at desktop, but the composition could belong to any co-living brand, and the ProofBar's second beat repeats the hero instead of escalating it. The single biggest opportunity: put a real Kostella number in the opening frame.

## What's Working

**1. The scrim is engineered, not decorated.** Assessment B composited the actual decoded photo against the analytic gradient alpha and sampled only the text line boxes. At 1440: H1 **5.66:1** (needs 3), intro **7.85:1** (needs 4.5), eyebrow **9.39:1**. The left-weighted ramp keeps the right side readable as a photograph instead of flattening it. Setting the intro solid rather than translucent was the correct call.

**2. The copy would survive a copy director.** Mechanism before benefit. A falsifiable claim rather than an adjective. Real figures, no rounding. Register correct for tenants.

**3. The ProofBar's divider construction is structurally correct.** `bg-line` on the container plus `gap-px` on the grid means the 1px rules are the container showing through, so they resolve at both 2 and 4 columns with no per-item border rules and no fragile `last:border-0`.

## Priority Issues

### [P1] The intro drops below AA contrast at 390px
**What.** Assessment B measured the real composited backdrop at mobile: intro worst case **4.26:1 against a required 4.5:1**, and the eyebrow at exactly **4.50** — on the line. At 390 the text spans into the scrim's `alpha 0.35` end, where the desktop margin disappears. A code comment in `Hero.tsx` claims a 5.6:1 floor; that matches the 1440 H1 measurement and **does not hold at 390**. The comment was written from a desktop-only calculation and is wrong as stated.
**Why it matters.** This is a mobile-first market and PRODUCT.md names parents — older eyes — as the payers. The failure is photo-dependent, so swapping in real Kostella photography could make it worse rather than better.
**Fix.** Make the scrim viewport-aware: at `<lg` the text spans the full width, so the ramp should be vertical or near-uniform rather than left-weighted. Then re-measure and correct the comment.
**Command:** `/impeccable audit`

### [P0] The ProofBar leaves the page grid
**What.** Verified: it is the only content block in the codebase without the `wrap` utility. "2008" sits at x=24 while the H1 directly above sits at x=152 — a 128px break. `px-6` (24px) matches neither gutter (20px mobile / 32px desktop).
**Why it matters.** This strip is where a fraud-fearing parent decides whether this looks like a real company. An alignment break in the first scroll reads as sloppiness before anyone reads a number — and the brand argument is "we are not sloppy operators."
**Fix.** Wrap the `<ul>` in `wrap`; keep `border-y` full-bleed if the band is wanted, but put the *content* on the 1200px column.
**Command:** `/impeccable layout`

### [P0] The opening frame contains no Kostella number
**What.** Zero uses of `numeral` in `Hero.tsx`, verified. The brand's identity face first appears at 32px in the ProofBar — below the desktop fold and two screens down on mobile.
**Why it matters.** This is the single reason the hero is interchangeable. Pitch material must put the uncopyable thing in frame one; right now frame one is a lounge photo the product doc admits is a placeholder.
**Fix.** Either replace the photograph with a facade frame where the real house-number plate is legible, or promote one figure into the left block at display scale.
**Command:** `/impeccable bolder`

### [P1] The ProofBar spends half its payload repeating the hero
**What.** Within ~800 vertical pixels: "2008" twice, "dikelola sendiri" three times, "31 gedung" twice. Only "340 kamar" and "14 bln" are new.
**Why it matters.** A pitch's second beat must escalate. Verbatim repetition signals there is nothing further to say.
**Fix.** Strip the duplicated facts out of the hero copy and let the ProofBar own them, or replace the two duplicate cells with figures the hero doesn't state.
**Command:** `/impeccable distill`

### [P1] The differentiator is the least-emphasised element in the block
**What.** "benar-benar kosong hari ini" — the claim PRODUCT.md says no competitor can copy — is 17px/400, visually identical to the geography clause it shares a paragraph with, and arrives second in the sentence.
**Why it matters.** Hierarchy is the argument. The page gives equal weight to a boast (31 buildings, where Mamikos wins on volume) and a proof (availability is real, which Mamikos structurally cannot match).
**Fix.** Split the paragraph. Lead with the availability sentence at the 21px step; demote geography.
**Command:** `/impeccable typeset`

### [P2] "14 bln" is mis-set and mis-placed
**What.** The least-copyable stat sits in the lowest-read position (far right desktop, bottom-right mobile). "bln" renders inside the `numeral` span — Archivo at 125% width, weight 700 — so the abbreviation reads as a stretched word.
**Fix.** Move to position one or two; split the unit out of the numeral span or spell "bulan". Change "kamar" → "kamar dikelola".
**Command:** `/impeccable typeset`

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (first-timer).** The left block says what Kostella *is* and never what to *do* — no link, anchor, or CTA in the entire left column. The only action assumes Jordan already knows a budget and an area, but a first-timer's actual first question is "what does a kos near Trisakti cost?" and the hero asks *them* to answer it.

**Casey (distracted mobile, 390px).** Measured: the ProofBar begins at y=904 — entirely below the fold. Casey sees **zero proof on the first screen**: a dark ceiling photo, three lines of type, and the top of a form. Nothing above the fold is tappable except the header CTA, which resolves to `#`. Slider thumb is 22×22, below the WCAG 2.2 minimum of 24×24; chips are 34.8px tall, clearing 24 but not 44.

**Parent / payer.** The four figures they need are two screens down on mobile and 128px off-grid on desktop, and **none carries an as-of date, source, or address**. "31 gedung" is exactly the shape of claim readme.md:24 says reads as fraud when unverifiable — and it is stated twice while neither section names a single building, street, or phone number. The 13px `ink-soft` labels are the smallest type on the page at the moment an older reader is trying hardest.

## Minor Observations

- H1 renders at 60px; the type scale defines H1 as 44px with no 60px step.
- Two measure systems in one block: H1 `max-w-[15ch]`, intro `max-w-[480px]`.
- `Hero.tsx` hand-rolls `SectionEyebrow` because the primitive's `inverse` colour (`ink-faint` #9A9892) is too dark over a photo. Fix the primitive, not every call site.
- The ProofBar `<ul>` has no accessible name and no preceding heading — a screen reader announces "list, 4 items" with no context. A `<dl>` is arguably the truer structure.
- The hero photo carries descriptive alt but is a decorative background, and PRODUCT.md states it is not a Kostella property — so the alt text is also factually wrong. `alt=""` is more honest.
- Neither section carries a landmark label.
- At 390 the intro orphans "Kamar yang" at a line end; `text-pretty` would help.
- Heading outline is clean: exactly one h1, no skipped levels. No horizontal overflow at any width tested.

## Questions to Consider
